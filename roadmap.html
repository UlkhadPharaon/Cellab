Cellab v1 is live: a touch-free 3D cell explorer driven by your webcam and your hands. Celly is the second iteration, built with the same gesture pipeline and a real rigged 3D hand.

  • • Rigged GLB hand (GLTFLoader) driven by the same 21 landmarks, bones 00_WRIST → 20_PINKY_TIP
  • • Landmark smoothing filter for steadier skeletons
  • • Hand control on the guide and quiz pages, not only the 3D view
  • • Libraries + model bundled for full offline use
  • • French / English toggle
  • • Plant cell mode, organelle zoom, in-scene labels
  • • WebXR and mobile support
